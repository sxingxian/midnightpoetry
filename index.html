Midnight
Poetry
Studio

Ideas arrive late. This is where they learn to sound like poems.

Temporary page. Products are being built nearby.
When the day lets go of me,the quiet begins to talk.
Nobody watches the work at midnight,so it has no reason to lie.
These are the hours the world gave up.I took them, and they built me.
An idea comes like a lit windowin a city I thought was asleep.
I am not chasing the sound of a poem.I keep the lamp on until it finds me.
So I build the way a poem speaks:slowly, honestly, and after dark.

The hours

Late-night sessions for ideas that need quiet to arrive. Midnight is when the noise drops and the work gets honest.

The voice

Writing, design and tools that sound the way poems feel: spare, rhythmic and a little unexpected.

The products

Small, careful things are in the works. This page holds the place until they’re ready to share.

Still building, by moonlight.
